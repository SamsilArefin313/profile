My Projects

Intelligent Inspection System for Anomaly Detection in Mining Rail Infrastructure (Master's Thesis)

Problem: Underground mine rail systems need early warning of ceiling hazards and derailment.

Approach: Built a Raspberry Pi inspection unit using dual cameras, YOLOv5n, an MPU9250 IMU, LoRa, CRC checking, and MODBUS integration.

Result: Achieved 97% ceiling-hazard accuracy and derailment classification with 96% precision and 98% recall.

A Gaussian Process-Enhanced Non-Linear Function and Bayesian Convolution–Bayesian Long Term Short Memory Based Ultra-Wideband Range Error Mitigation Method for Line of Sight and Non-Line of Sight Scenarios. (Mathematics MDPI)

Problem: UWB ranging loses accuracy in multipath and non-line-of-sight environments.

Approach: Combined Gaussian Process bias correction with a Bayesian Conv-BLSTM and spatial-temporal attention.

Result: Produced a robust LOS/NLOS mitigation method with uncertainty estimation, published in Mathematics.

View publication (DOI)
Research on Vertical Shaft Detection System Based on CMOS-Camera and Lidar. (IEEE Access)

Problem: Conventional shaft inspection cannot provide timely warning of deformation and alignment hazards.

Approach: Integrated CMOS cameras, LiDAR, laser collimation, and ROS-based processing.

Result: Validated deformation, perpendicularity, and vibration measurements for intelligent shaft monitoring.

View publication (DOI)
